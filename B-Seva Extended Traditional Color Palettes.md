## Palette 4: Sacred Maroon & Ivory

### Core Colors
- **Deep Maroon**: `#800020` (RGB: 128, 0, 32)
- **Rich Ivory**: `#FFFFF0` (RGB: 255, 255, 240)
- **Bronze**: `#CD7F32` (RGB: 205, 127, 50)
- **Sandstone**: `#E6D5B8` (RGB: 230, 213, 184)
- **Burgundy**: `#6B0F1A` (RGB: 107, 15, 26)

### Supporting Colors
- **Light Maroon**: `#A0364A` (RGB: 160, 54, 74) - Hover states, accents
- **Warm Ivory**: `#FFF9E6` (RGB: 255, 249, 230) - Backgrounds
- **Light Bronze**: `#E6A85C` (RGB: 230, 168, 92) - Highlights
- **Success Green**: `#2E7D32` (RGB: 46, 125, 50) - Confirmations
- **Alert Crimson**: `#C62828` (RGB: 198, 40, 40) - Warnings

### Rationale
Maroon holds deep significance in Indian culture, representing devotion, sacrifice, and spiritual depth. It is commonly seen in temple architecture, traditional attire, and religious ceremonies. Paired with rich ivory, this palette creates an elegant, timeless aesthetic that conveys reverence and premium quality. Bronze adds warmth and traditional craftsmanship associations, while sandstone provides earthy grounding. This palette is ideal for B-Seva if the brand wants to emphasize devotional service, traditional values, and cultural depth with a sophisticated, heritage-rich appearance.

### Application Guidelines
- **Website**: Maroon headers and navigation, bronze CTAs, ivory content backgrounds, sandstone for cards
- **Mobile App**: Deep maroon app bars, bronze action buttons, ivory content areas
- **Marketing**: Maroon dominance for gravitas, bronze for warmth and approachability
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AAA compliant with burgundy text on ivory (contrast ratio 14.2:1)

## Comparison with Original Traditional Spiritual Palette

All four new palettes maintain the core principles of the Traditional Spiritual palette:

1. **Cultural Authenticity**: Each palette draws from traditional Indian colors and cultural symbolism
2. **Warm-Cool Balance**: Pairing warm accent colors with cooler neutrals for visual harmony
3. **Premium Quality**: Incorporating metallic or rich accent colors (bronze, gold) for elevated perception
4. **Light Backgrounds**: Using cream, ivory, or pale tones for excellent readability and reduced eye strain
5. **Accessibility**: Meeting or exceeding WCAG AA standards for digital accessibility
6. **Versatility**: Complete color systems suitable for websites, mobile apps, and all marketing materials

### Selection Guidance

- **Palette 4 (Maroon & Ivory)**: Choose for devotional emphasis, heritage luxury, sophisticated tradition
- **Palette 5 (Green & Saffron)**: Choose for environmental focus, balanced energy, holistic service approach
- **Palette 6 (Purple & Gold)**: Choose for premium positioning, spiritual wisdom, transformative services
- **Palette 7 (Terracotta & Cream)**: Choose for grassroots authenticity, warm approachability, handcrafted quality
