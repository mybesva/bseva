B-SEVA Logo Variants - Design Concepts

Reference Design Analysis:
- Letter "B" integrated with Om symbol (ॐ)
- Color scheme: Navy blue (#2C3E50) and orange (#F39C12)
- Modern, clean design with spiritual elements
- Text: "B-SEVA" with B in navy, SEVA in orange

Logo Variant Concepts:

1. GANESH-INCORPORATED B (Primary Request)
   - Letter "B" shaped to incorporate Ganesh's silhouette
   - Ganesh's trunk forming the curves of the B
   - Crown/head at top of B, body integrated into letter form
   - Same color palette: navy blue and orange
   - "B-SEVA" text below in same style

2. LOTUS PETAL B
   - Letter "B" formed with lotus petal motifs
   - Petals creating the curves and structure
   - Spiritual symbolism through lotus design
   - Navy and orange color scheme maintained
   - "B-SEVA" text below

3. DIYA FLAME B
   - Letter "B" integrated with traditional diya (lamp) flame
   - Flame elements forming the upper curve
   - Base of diya forming lower curve
   - Represents service and enlightenment
   - Navy and orange colors
   - "B-SEVA" text below

4. MINIMALIST GEOMETRIC B
   - Clean, modern geometric interpretation
   - Abstract spiritual elements (mandala patterns)
   - Simplified curves with sacred geometry
   - Navy and orange palette
   - "B-SEVA" text below
