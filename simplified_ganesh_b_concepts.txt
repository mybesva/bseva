Simplified Ganesh-B Logo Variants for B-Seva

Current Design Analysis:
- Detailed Ganesh figure with full body, trunk, crown, ears
- Complex curves and multiple elements
- Rich gradients and detailed features
- Full sitting Ganesh pose integrated into B

Simplified Variant Concepts:

Variant 1: ABSTRACT TRUNK B
- Focus only on Ganesh's iconic trunk forming the B curves
- Minimal crown element at top
- Single curved trunk line creating both B curves
- Reduced detail, cleaner lines
- Flat colors or subtle gradients
- More geometric, less illustrative

Variant 2: OUTLINE SILHOUETTE B
- Simple outline/line art style
- Ganesh head profile forming upper B
- Minimal trunk curve for lower B
- No fill, just navy outlines with orange accents
- Very clean, modern line work
- Reduced complexity while maintaining recognition

Variant 3: GEOMETRIC GANESH B
- Highly simplified geometric shapes
- Circular head, triangular crown
- Simple curved trunk as B element
- Flat design with no gradients
- Bold, clean shapes
- Modern, icon-like appearance
