2. Glossary of Ritual Terms & Samagri Meanings
Understanding the spiritual and practical significance of each item ensures B-Seva can educate its customers and provide culturally accurate kits.
Havan Samagri: A sacred, coarsely powdered mixture of dried herbs, medicinal roots, leaves, seeds, and fragrant items (like guggul, loban, and sandalwood). Offered into the fire (Agni), it is believed to purify the environment and act as food for the divine.
Samidha (Dry Woods): Specific dried twigs and wood pieces used to ignite and sustain the sacred fire. Often sourced from sacred trees like Mango, Peepal, Banyan, or Palash, they represent the physical body being offered to the divine.
Navagraha Samidha: A specific set of 9 different types of wood sticks, each corresponding to one of the nine celestial planets (Navagraha). Used in astrological pacification rituals.
Purna Ahuti Silk Cloth: A piece of fine cloth (often silk) used in the final, grand oblation (Purna Ahuti) of a homa. It signifies complete surrender and the successful culmination of the ritual.
Dharba / Kusha Grass: A highly purifying sacred grass used extensively in Vedic rituals to make rings (Pavitram) for the priest and participants, and as seating mats. It is believed to block negative energies and radiation.
Pure Cow Ghee: Clarified butter derived from indigenous cow milk. It is the primary fuel for the homa fire, symbolizing purity, nourishment, and spiritual illumination.
Akshata: Unbroken rice grains mixed with turmeric. Used universally as a substitute for any missing offering and to bestow blessings.
Kalash: A metal or earthen pot filled with water, topped with mango leaves and a coconut. It represents the universe, the body of the deity, and the source of life (Amrita).
Pinda: Balls made of cooked rice, barley flour, and black sesame seeds. Offered during Shradh and death rituals to provide spiritual nourishment to departed ancestors.
Tarpanam: The act of offering water mixed with black sesame seeds to satisfy the souls of the deceased ancestors (Pitrus).
Ganga Jal: Holy water from the Ganges River, used universally for purification (Shuddhikaran) of the self, the space, and the ritual items.
Kanya Daan Samagri: Items used during a wedding when giving away the bride. It usually includes specific grains, coins, turmeric, and textiles.
Vastram: Clothing or fabric offered to the deity, symbolizing respect, modesty, and the covering of the soul with divine grace.
